Recording a meeting or webinar
  1. Meeting in your browser (Google Meet, Zoom or Teams on the web): pick the Chrome tab with the meeting and switch on "Also share tab audio".
  2. Zoom or Teams app on Windows: pick Entire screen and switch on "Also share system audio".
  3. On a Mac, only tab sound can be captured, so join the meeting in your browser.
  4. Wear headphones so speakers' voices aren't picked up twice by your mic.
  5. For long sessions, set Save recordings to → My folder so the video is written to disk while it records.
